the garden

paint/garden.planes — a scene that lives. Sun and moon, weather that drifts on its own noise, plants that grow and flower, bees by day and fireflies by night, and ambient notes in exact ratios. The whole thing is a pure function of two inputs: the tick and the seed. Press play, or drag anywhere in the three days. Click anything — the sun, a flower, a bee, the rain — to ask where it came from.

Serving: this page fetches paint/garden.planes and imports js/*.mjs as ES modules — both are refused from a file:// origin. Serve the repo root (python3 -m http.server 8000) and open http://localhost:8000/garden.html. No bundler, no transpiler, no network dependency beyond that server.

Purity. The picture and the notes at a given (tick, seed) pair are always the same picture and the same notes — play forward to a tick or drag straight to it and the frame is identical, because every run starts from state: nothing and reads nothing else. Saving. SVG and PNG capture the frame shown right now, not a fresh run — the same "what's on screen" rule paint.html's exporters follow. Sound fires only while playing: never while scrubbing, and never on the export path.

Asking performs nothing. A question reads the derivation the run already produced. The effect surface beside the canvas is computed from the source without running it, and does not change when you click.

After an update. The .planes fetch carries a per-visit token and is never served stale. The .mjs module graph cannot: if a browser ever serves a mix of old and new modules, the output pane says so and names the fix — in Safari, Develop → Empty Caches, because a plain reload keeps ES modules.
