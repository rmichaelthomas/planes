Serving: this page fetches paint/garden.planes and imports js/*.mjs as ES modules, so it needs a static server (python3 -m http.server 8000) and no network beyond it.

Purity. The picture and the notes at a given (tick, seed) are always the same picture and the same notes — play forward to a tick or drag straight to it and the frame is identical. Asking performs nothing: a question reads the derivation the run already produced, and the surface above is computed without running a line.

Saving. SVG and PNG capture the frame on screen, not a fresh run. Sound fires only while playing or when you click a mark — never while scrubbing, never on export.
