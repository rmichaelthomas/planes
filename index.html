Opens directly in Safari or Firefox. Chrome blocks ES-module loading over file:// as a security policy — for Chrome, serve the folder with python3 -m http.server and open http://localhost:8000/. No bundler, no transpiler: the page loads the real js/*.mjs modules and the real grammar/*.json as JSON modules.
