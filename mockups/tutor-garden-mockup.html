# a garden that is a pure function of one number
use draw
use sound

let seed = 481027
let ground = 0.28 because "dark enough that the leaves read as light"

# value noise: two corners, blended with a cosine
to blend of a, b, t:
  let smooth = (1 - cosine of (t * 180)) / 2
  give a + (b - a) * smooth

to growth of column, day:
  let x = column * 0.7 + day * 0.02
  give blend of (corner of x), (corner of (x + 1)), (part of x)

to plant of column, day:
  let g = growth of column, day
  let height = 40 + g * 140 because "tall enough to catch the light, short enough to stay in frame"
  stem of column, height
  # the same g picks the note
  note of (220 * (interval of g))

for each column in [0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8]:
  plant of column, day
why

Click any plant in the garden.

It will tell you where its height came from — one step at a time, down to the number you chose. And the note you hear is the same number.

Mockup only — nothing here runs Planes. The chain, the surface pane and the why line format mirror what the real page would compute. Just intonation throughout: every interval is an exact ratio, so a fifth is 3/2 and stays 3/2.
